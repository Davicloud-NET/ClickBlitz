# ClickBlitz Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

ClickBlitz is an auto-clicker for Windows published by David Jonjic. This policy applies to every way of getting it: the Microsoft Store version and the downloads from GitHub.

## Summary

ClickBlitz does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no telemetry, and it never connects to the internet.

## What ClickBlitz does on your device

- **Settings.** ClickBlitz saves the settings you choose (click speed, what to click, the hotkey and its mode) in a file named `ClickBlitz.ini`. The Microsoft Store version keeps this file in the application data folder Windows provides for the app. The portable version keeps it next to the executable. The file stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.
- **Keyboard and mouse.** To start and stop when you press your hotkey, ClickBlitz asks Windows whether that one key or mouse button is pressed. While you are choosing a key, it reads the key you press in its own window. It does not record, log or store what you type or click, in ClickBlitz or in any other program.
- **Clicks and key presses it generates.** ClickBlitz sends the mouse clicks or key presses you configured to Windows, which delivers them to whatever is under the cursor or has the keyboard focus. ClickBlitz does not read the contents of other windows.

## Data shared with third parties

None. ClickBlitz has no network functionality, so it cannot send data to the publisher or to anyone else.

## The stores you download it from

The Microsoft Store and GitHub are operated by Microsoft and GitHub, not by the publisher of ClickBlitz, and process data under their own privacy statements when you download or update the app. Microsoft may provide the publisher with aggregated statistics about the Store version, such as the number of downloads and crash reports. These do not identify you.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version removes its settings file. For the portable version, delete `ClickBlitz.ini` from the folder that contains `ClickBlitz.exe`.

## Children

ClickBlitz does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of ClickBlitz changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
